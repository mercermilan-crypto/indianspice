About Us

Experience the Essence of India

✦

Since 2020, Indian Spice has brought the soul of Indian home cooking to the heart of Ontario. From bold Punjabi curries to the delicate art of South Indian dosas, every dish is made with whole spices, fresh ingredients, and the kind of care you'd find in a family kitchen.

With locations in Hamilton and Waterloo, we serve the favorites of two regions side by side — the smoky tandoor of the north, the crisp rava and chutneys of the south, and the unmistakable street food that ties them together.
